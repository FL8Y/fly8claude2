## 1. Brief before generation

Create one original Android app-cloning icon for FLY8. It must communicate distinct app instances, not generic technology. Retain the established near-black and cyan-blue palette, create a distinct silhouette, and remove both failure patterns from the earlier attempts: generic overlapping glass panels and a crowded anime/cyberpunk scene.

Reference hierarchy: current user requirements > verified product category / Android launcher constraints > established FLY8 palette > mood cues from the reference art. The reference eye is allowed to inform abstract geometry but must not be copied as a literal eye, character, or screenshot.

## 2. Concept council: route selection

Scores below are comparative design hypotheses before generation, not image ratings.

| Route | Product meaning | Ownability | Silhouette | Small-size potential | Primary risk |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| A. Forked aperture / echo iris | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Can read as a camera or biometric scanner. |
| B. F/8-derived abstract monogram | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Can become a generic lettermark or look like a camera aperture. |
| C. Split-core / dual-channel mark | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | Can read as network sync if the branch is too literal. |
| D. One path resolving into two channels | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | Can resemble branching/network infrastructure. |

**Selected route: C, using the clarity of D.** Construct one custom, asymmetrical mark from one primary body and one subordinate echo path. The branch must imply one context becoming two independent instances, but must not use an arrow, cloud, standard synchronization glyph, or stacked cards. Use a central negative-space core so the silhouette is ownable. Do not force an eye or an “8” into it; those are optional associations, not mandatory literal drawings.

## 3. Prompt QA before generation

### Pass A — traceability

- Product: explicitly names an Android cloning utility and separate app instances.
- Symbol: specifies one primary custom body, a fork/echo, and central negative space.
- Identity: explicitly rejects the earlier generic overlap and crowded eye/UI design.
- Color: exact FLY8 colors and restricted roles.
- Framing: plain white presentation canvas, distinct centered dark squircle tile, 1:1.
- Small format: safe margins and conceptual readability at 24/32/48 dp.
- Negative prompt: no words, fake text, anime character, windows/dialogs, stock symbols, watermarks, clutter, or heavy effects.
- Honesty: does not label the generated raster image an SVG or tested Android asset.

### Pass B — contradictions and predictable failures

| Predicted failure | Prompt-level correction |
|---|---|
| Model renders generic stacked panels | Explicitly forbid overlapping squares/cards and define the actual path construction. |
| Symbol reads as sync/network | Prohibit arrows and standard sync glyphs; integrate the branch into one compact body. |
| Symbol reads as a literal eye/camera | Prohibit an eyeball, iris drawing, lens rings, and biometric-reader imagery. |
| Too much detail at launcher size | One main mass, one echo only, no hairlines, no particles, no decorative UI. |
| Effects hide weak geometry | Flat vector-like fills; no gradient, shadow, bevel, lens flare, or glow. |
| White field becomes part of icon | Explicitly separate white presentation canvas from the actual dark tile. |
| Fake wordmark appears | No letters, words, numbers, captions, or pseudo-type inside the image. |

### Pass C — five-role prompt council

This is a simulated multi-lens review by one operator, not a claim of five independent human reviewers.

- **Brand strategist:** Strength: product context is explicit. Risk: abstract branch might still feel like a network tool. Patch: require a compact local fork inside one recognizable mark, not a sprawling path diagram.
- **Identity designer:** Strength: one mass plus one echo can be memorable. Risk: dual-channel abstract symbols can be generic. Patch: asymmetrical proportions and an intentional, distinctive core; test silhouette with colors removed.
- **Android launcher specialist:** Strength: one centered 1:1 tile and small-size targets are specified. Risk: tight counters may close at 24 dp. Patch: prohibit narrow gaps and keep the interior core broad and simple.
- **Art director / production reviewer:** Strength: tightly controlled palette and flat geometry. Risk: the generator may sneak in glossy depth or glows. Patch: make flat vector-like rendering dominant and explicitly exclude glow/shadows/gradients.
- **Skeptical user:** Strength: concept no longer depends on a face or several windows. Risk: the meaning could remain abstract. Patch: make the primary-to-secondary split legible in the silhouette rather than adding literal app windows.

## 4. Exact image-generation prompt for Run 01

**Role and objective**

Act as a senior brand identity designer for premium Android utility applications. Design one original, distinctive launcher icon for FLY8 Clone, an Android app-cloning utility for separate app instances and parallel contexts. The outcome must look deliberately designed by a professional identity designer, not like a generic AI-generated technology icon. Treat the product meaning, the shape, and the negative space as the core; effects and decoration are not a substitute for an idea.

**Concept**

Create a custom abstract **split-core / dual-channel mark**. One compact, continuous primary form should separate into two clearly independent channels or paths, expressing that a single app context can have a distinct second instance. Integrate the split into the main silhouette so it feels like one ownable symbol rather than several objects placed together. Use a central, generous negative-space core to create a memorable internal shape. Allow a slight intentional asymmetry to make the construction distinctive.

The primary form and its echo must have a clear hierarchy: one dominant body, one subordinate duplicate path, no more. The branch should be local and compact, not a network diagram. It may subtly evoke an aperture or a duplicated core, but it must not become a literal human eye, camera lens, iris scanner, infinity sign, arrow, sync symbol, F/8 text, or standard clone glyph. Do not force the mark to resemble a letter or a number. The result should be identifiable as a single, simple silhouette before color or internal details are noticed.

**Make it ownable**

Do not use two equal rounded rectangles, two overlapping squares, app-window cards, folders, clouds, chain links, generic infinity loops, common camera apertures, generic AI sparkles, or the default “two copies” pictogram. Do not solve originality by adding more parts. Originality must come from the geometry, proportion, asymmetry, and negative-space relationship. If the symbol still resembles an ordinary sync/network icon, change the structural shape.

**Tile and presentation**

Use one plain, pure-white square presentation canvas. Center one single dark rounded-square/squircle app tile on that canvas, with a generous even white margin around it. The tile itself is the app icon field and must be near-black. Do not confuse the white outer canvas with the tile background. Inside the tile, center one symbol and maintain a large, safe negative-space margin so common Android launcher masks cannot clip it. The logo should occupy roughly 58–64% of the tile's width, optically adjusted for balance. Keep the tile and mark clean, crisp, and unambiguous.

**Color system**

Use only a disciplined subset of FLY8's palette:
- Tile: near-black #000001 or deep surface #010205.
- Primary mark: cyan-blue #176E94.
- Secondary structural area, only if essential: deep blue #01304C or #01132A.
- Optional tiny crisp contrast accent: cool white #FCFDFD.

Prefer two flat colors plus the dark tile. No pink for this core mark. No rainbow, purple/pink AI gradient, metallic colors, or random secondary accents. The cyan should define the memorable form, while dark negative space does real structural work. The mark must remain understandable if converted to one solid color or grayscale.

**Rendering style**

Near-flat, crisp, vector-like geometry, controlled stroke/contour weight, consistent joins and radii, clean edges, intentional spacing, strong shape hierarchy. Premium through precision and restraint, not gloss. Do not use a gradient, drop shadow, glow, bloom, bevel, glass texture, chrome, realistic 3D render, lens flare, film grain, scratches, outer rings, or light streaks. Any highlight that is not necessary for the shape is unnecessary and should not be present.

**Launcher-size rules**

Make the key visual relationship survive at 24, 32, and 48 dp. Use broad counters and clear gaps. Do not make tiny lines, specks, fine circuits, particles, or delicate outlines necessary to the meaning. The mark should look balanced beside ordinary Android app icons and remain contained under rounded-square and circular crops. It should also pass a single-color silhouette review without relying on the specific cyan hue.

**Strict exclusions**

No text, letters, numbers, captions, pseudo-letters, typography, slogans, watermarks, UI screenshots, desktop dialogs, title bars, buttons, app-window stacks, multiple logo options, decorative framing, anime characters, human faces, hair, literal eyeballs, biometric scanner art, camera icons, folder/cloud icons, chain links, sync arrows, generic infinity marks, floating objects, arbitrary circuit lines, random particles, excessive glow, gradients, drop shadows, glassmorphism, realistic 3D, noisy detail, busy cyberpunk background, copied brand marks, or cropped shapes.

**Output contract**

One original app icon only, 1:1 square composition, clean white outer canvas, one centered dark rounded-square tile, one self-contained mark, no text or additional objects, high-resolution crisp edges, plain presentation, generous safe area. The result is a raster concept image unless an actual vector/export step is completed afterward.

**Critique before accepting the result**

Check the silhouette first. If the mark reads as stacked squares, camera, biometric reader, generic sync/network, or an AI icon, reject it and revise the structural geometry. If any detail fails to survive at launcher size, simplify it. If the white canvas/tile boundary is unclear, correct the composition. If the mark works only because of lighting, remove the lighting and rebuild the form. Prefer a genuinely new shape over decorating a weak first result.
