## Design intent

Build a distinct, professional Android cloning-app identity. Preserve a near-black and cyan-blue palette, use an original product-specific symbol, and prioritize recognition at launcher-icon sizes. The earlier overlapping-glass-squares concept is explicitly not the target: it is too generic. The reference image's anime eye and cyberpunk UI may inform the mood, but should not be copied literally or reproduced as a crowded miniature screenshot.

## Quality rule

Aim for 5/5 across the defined scorecard, but never declare a design perfect by assertion. Score it against evidence, perform an adversarial review, revise identifiable weaknesses, and report the remaining caveats honestly.
