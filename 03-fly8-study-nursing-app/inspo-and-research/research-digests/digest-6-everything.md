# Digest 6: agents-playground / everything

## 1. What these sources are
Source root R=.../mainview/notes/agents-playground (all paths below are relative to it). It is the "agents' playground" of the repo FL8Y/readthischatgpt: a boundary-rule README, a generic autonomous-agent master prompt (`AGENT_MASTER_PROMPT.md`, process doc, no nursing content), and `everything/`, a non-destructive consolidation of the output of 26 earlier agent workspaces. OBSERVED: it is mostly a **preservation and audit archive**, not a content library. Real nursing content is 32 transcribed study notes (about 101k words total, 0.6k to 7k words each) plus 4 curated guides (about 6k words). The other 113 archived files (145 under `archive/agent-records/`) are handoffs, coverage checklists, research logs, work queues and reference lists. I skimmed the archive by file names and read the index, the review files, and 3 notes; I did not read all 32. Trust: structure and counts are well documented and byte-verified (SHA match). Clinical accuracy is NOT verified.

## 2. Key facts
- **Source of the notes** (OBSERVED, `notes/mental-health/...pp56-58.md`): user-supplied photographs of a printed study guide ("Power Development Center for Training, Study & Review, Inc."), numbered by printed page. Agents transcribed each page, then added "ADDED EXPLANATION / EXTERNAL CROSS-CHECK" sections, separate from the source paraphrase.
- **Coverage by subject** (`everything/INDEX.md`): Fundamentals 4 notes (pp. 9-11, 17-19, 32-34, p. 60 abdominal regions); Community health 3 (pp. 38-43, 83-85); Infectious diseases 4 (pp. 68-82); Maternal-child 4 (pp. 23-31, p. 61 reflexes); Med-surg and perioperative/pain 6 (pp. 62-64, 89-101); Meds/labs 3 plus toxicology 1 (pp. 14-16, 44-46, 50-52, 59); Mental health 2 (pp. 53-58); Theory/ethics/law 5 (pp. 1-13). Printed pages covered are roughly 1-101 with gaps (about pp. 20-22, 35-37, 47-49, 65-67, 77-79, 86-88 absent).
- **4 curated guides** (`everything/curated/`): mental health pp. 53-58; perioperative/postoperative/pain pp. 89-101; communicable disease and public health pp. 68-85; professional foundations pp. 1-13. They are synthesis derivatives that link their source notes.
- **Verification status** (`ARCHIVE_VERIFICATION.md`, `README.md`): byte-level preservation passed (145 of 145 archived blobs match, 0 mismatches). Every note carries an appended caution: "AI-written study note ... Not nurse-reviewed". Clinical and legal review is explicitly NOT done.
- **Self-reported defects** (`review/CONTENT_CONFLICTS.md`): the source transcription mislabels RA 10352 as an alcohol/tobacco excise law (Lawphil says it is the 2013 General Appropriations Act; RA 10351 is the excise law). AO 2007-0012 (drinking water) is superseded by AO 2017-0010. EO 352 is cited as the FHSIS basis but is unresolved. The p. 101 pain-management note has an unreadable medication name.
- **Source-table problems the agents flagged** (`notes/medications-and-toxicology/...p59.md`): old handout items are outdated or unsafe, e.g. cyanide antidote listed as amyl nitrite (hydroxocobalamin preferred), TCA overdose listing physostigmine (bicarbonate is the key treatment), and a "18 doses every 4 hours" NAC regimen. The note says "Never apply doses from this old table". The notes explicitly keep source-vs-correction distinctions, which is useful for question-writing.
- **Conflict register finds no confirmed cross-note contradictions** (`review/CONTENT_CONFLICTS.md`), but says the check is not exhaustive. `DUPLICATES_AND_OVERLAPS.md` finds adjacent-page topics are complementary, with no confirmed exact duplicates.
- Many notes end with a short "Retrieval check" Q&A (23 of 32 notes), plus tables and "high-yield distinctions" lists. These are the closest thing to question material (recall prompts, not MCQs).

## 3. REUSE
- **Overlap-resolving cross-check data**: the flagged errors above (RA 10352, AO 2007-0012, toxicology corrections) should be checked against FLY8's presets; they are a ready-made "known-wrong handout facts" list.
- **MCQ source material** (INFERRED): 23 "Retrieval check" lists (about 5-10 recall questions each, roughly 150 stems) and the "high-yield distinctions" bullets can seed stems/answers. Densest, best-suited notes: drug toxicities/antidotes p. 59 (17 rows: drug to antidote, with modern corrections, ideal for matching-style distractors); lab values pp. 44-52; communicable diseases pp. 68-85 (transmission, precautions, drug of choice); psychiatric pp. 53-58 (positive vs negative symptoms, anxiolytics); delegation, 5 and 10 rights, clinical pathways pp. 32-34 (matches the PNLE delegation/management archetype); Piaget/Kohlberg, milestones and reflexes pp. 26-31/61; Philippine laws pp. 3-5 (only after the legal fixes). Rationale text is already present in the "Added explanation" columns.
- **Pattern worth copying**: the source-vs-added-explanation split, "uncertainty audit" per note, and "do not infer" handling of unreadable text. This fits FLY8's "mark AI-added facts for textbook check" rule.
- `MIGRATION_MANIFEST.csv` (32 rows: source path, SHA, topic, page range, relationship) is a handy index mapping each note to printed pages and clusters. `SOURCE_FILE_INVENTORY.csv` (147 rows) is audit trail only; skip.

## 4. DO NOT REUSE
- `AGENT_MASTER_PROMPT.md`, README, WORK_QUEUE/handoff/log/checklist files and `archive/agent-records/*` (145 files): process records, no study value. Duplicates of the 32 notes also sit in the archive (pre-caution originals); do not double count.
- Verbatim handout wording: the notes are paraphrases of a commercial review center's printed study guide. Treat the PDF/photo origin as copyrighted and re-express rather than copy; the same stance FLY8 took (0.3% 10-gram overlap rewrite).
- Any dose, range, antidote or Philippine law number as-is (outdated, unreviewed, partially known wrong). The unreadable p. 101 medication name: never infer it.
- Nothing here conflicts with "no flashcards": the notes themselves contain no flashcard system. Only use "Retrieval check" items by converting them to MCQ with rationales, not as cards.
